## 🔬 About & Research

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science & Engineering at **[IIIT Hyderabad](https://www.iiit.ac.in/)**, advised by **[Dr. Vineet Gandhi](https://faculty.iiit.ac.in/~vgandhi/)** at the Centre for Visual Information Technology (CVIT).

My research centers on **Speech AI & Multimodal Synthesis**:
- **Atypical Speech Restoration**: Real-time acoustic restoration of dysarthric and impaired speech using discrete neural acoustic units and low-latency autoregressive decoders.
- **Silent Speech Interfaces (SSI)**: Synthesizing intelligible speech directly from non-invasive tongue ultrasound video sequences via spatiotemporal transformers.
- **High-Performance ML Systems**: Low-latency on-device inference, SIMD-accelerated kernels (AVX2), and systems programming in **Rust, C++, and PyTorch**.
